Structured AI Agent for Prior Authorization
Problem Statement
Prior Authorization (PA) is an administrative procedure in health insurance where healthcare providers must collect the necessary clinical evidence and supporting documentation for specific medical treatments to secure coverage under a patient's health plan.
This process is:
The administrative process is characterized by repetitive, time-intensive manual tasks.
The process involves navigating the complex landscape of the fragmented U.S. market, which comprises regional and national health plans with distinct prior-authorization rules. This complexity frequently results in care denials and incomplete submissions.
Ultimately, this administrative load diverts critical resources away from patients and causes significant delays in treatment.
The core challenge is not clinical judgment — it is the structured assembly and validation of required evidence against the patient’s unique health plan.

Medical Claim Process (Revenue Cycle Management) Overview

The Medical Claims Process typically includes:

Patient registration and insurance verification
Medical coding and charge capture
Claim submission through electronic systems
Payer adjudication and review
Payment posting and reconciliation
Denial management and appeals
Medical claim clearinghouses or medical revenue management companies provide end-to-end services to manage the full medical claim reimbursement lifecycle.

User Stories
Hospital System and Physician Groups (Provider)
It is challenging for healthcare professionals to navigate different regional and national health plans with distinct prior-authorization rules. The documentation and back-and-forth communication required by prior authorization generate an immense administrative burden and lead to clinicians' burnout. 

Persona: "As a clinician, I want an agent to request a prior authorization check based on my patient’s health plan. The agent will autofill the eligibility criteria/ questionnaire based on the electronic health record and prompt for additional documentation for unmet criteria.
Key Agentic Task: extract clinical evidence from the FHIR-based data and map it to the eligibility requirements from the coverage policy, identify the documentation gap, and generate submission-ready bundle.
Value Proposition: Faster “time-to-treatment” and reduced claim denial

Claims Clearinghouse and Revenue Cycle Management
RCM companies often act as an outsourced "back office" for healthcare providers. They enable providers to navigate the thousands of health plans in the US healthcare market. It’s challenging to keep track of the constantly changing coverage policies of health plans offered by national and regional payers and to transmit data with specific EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) formats.

Persona: "As an RCM Manager, I want an agent to pre-scrub authorizations for 'Technical Denials' (e.g., missing NPI, incorrect CPT code, or expired eligibility) before they reach the payer, so that I can maintain a 98% first-pass approval rate."
Key Agentic Task: takes the FHIR-based data and maps it into the specific EDI formats required by thousands of different regional payers.
Value Proposition: Faster “time-to-payment” and reduced claims denial
Health Insurance (Payer)
Payers are overwhelmed by the volume of manual reviews while facing increasingly stringent decision timeframes and quality regulatory requirements. Their primary pain point is the “medical necessity” review, which requires healthcare professionals to audit within a given timeframe. 

Persona: "As a Payer Medical Director, I want an agent that automatically extracts clinical biomarkers from a provider's EHR, so that I can instantly approve 80% of routine requests without a manual nurse review."
Key Agentic Task: The agent validates the prior authorization request, identifies the eligibility gap, prompts manual review, and drafts a denial letter. 
Value Proposition: Reduced administrative spend (MLR), higher member satisfaction.

Reasoning Framework
To achieve a semi-automated prior authorization request with a human-in-the-loop validation process, we propose a reasoning framework that includes the following steps: 

Check Prior auth requirement: input patient_id, CPT code, or NDC code
Search for relevant health plan policy, turned into an eligibility criteria checklist
Query the EHR in accordance with the checklist/ questionnaire
Synthesize the coverage requirement and relevant patient records/evidence
Returned the structured prompts for the clinicians to answer for the unmet criteria

Export the bundled submission package, including the clinical evidence (FHIR sources)
